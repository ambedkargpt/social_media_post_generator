Use the system instructions. Everything you need for this generation is in the JSON object below.

Field guide:
- user_profile: the author you are writing as. Highest authority on tone, length and format.
- news: the story being responded to.
- video: the source video for this story. source_url is the link it came from.
- source_video_chunks: passages from THIS story's own video. These are the speaker's words about this story, and they may be used for facts.
- ideology_chunks: passages retrieved from OTHER videos. They are your ideological and philosophical grounding only. Take arguments, framing and Ambedkarite reasoning from them. NEVER take a fact, figure, date, place name or event from them: they describe a different story, and using their detail here attributes one event's facts to another.
- research: verified findings from outside sources.
    - research.use_these: build the post on these. Each carries a verdict, an instruction in how_to_treat, and the facts with their sources.
    - research.do_not_contradict: verified findings that do not help our argument. Do not build on them, and never state anything that contradicts them.
    - research.stance_mode "angle" means lead with what supports our case. It never means state something the evidence contradicts.
- If research is null, no outside verification was available. Ground the post in the news and the chunks alone, and claim nothing beyond them.
