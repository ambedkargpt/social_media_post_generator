AmbedkarGPT/App overhaul/Rev. 2 · checked against the design brief

Make the whole app look like Bhim Radio.

Your brief points to the one screen that already works: a flat, sharp broadcast desk with one accent color and no glass. Neela Press applies that same approach to every screen after the landing page, and adds three things the brief asks for: type that reads well in Hindi first, a light paper mode for reading, and nothing decorative that a cheap Android phone has to pay for.

01

How this meets the brief

§2.1 Hindi is first-classAll three typefaces carry Devanagari and Latin, so no heading ever falls back to the OS font. There is a Hindi version of the dashboard below the English one that tests them at heading size. Keep the existing ~12% size bump for small Hindi text. No label depends on UPPERCASE, since Hindi has no capital letters, and every button is sized to fit the Hindi text.
§2.2 Reduced motionEvery animation proposed here, including the chakra, the "print" reveal and the stamps, has a still fallback. The 17 prefers-reduced-motion blocks stay as they are.
§2.3 Dark only → two modesThe app stays dark for working screens. The second mode is paper (#F3EBDD), and it's used where people read: post previews, drafts, BhimBot answers, and the corpus later on. It starts small, on 4 surfaces, rather than as a full light theme across every screen.
§2.4 Mid-range AndroidNo orbs, no backdrop-blur, no glow shadows, no gradient text. Surfaces are solid fills with 1–2px lines. The only shadow left is the one under paper cards, and it's static.
§5 The "AI look"0 Sparkles icons (down from 32): the primary button uses an arrow or the chakra. Corners follow a strict ladder, not "round everything": 16px for cards, 12px for paper, 8–10px for buttons and inputs, and circles only for avatars, party logos and the chakra. There are no pill shapes, which replaces the 213 rounded-full. Hover darkens the color or moves it 1px down, so it feels pressed instead of glowing. GlassCard and .btn-gradient are removed.
§7 Carry the weightCondensed, poster-style headlines, serif for news and reading, newspaper-style rules between rows, stamps for status, and the real party symbols (the hand, the cycle, the lotus) where a story belongs to a party. It should feel like a movement press, not a SaaS product. "POLITICS DIVIDES. CONSTITUTION UNITES." becomes black poster type with no gradient.

The ground moves from saturated navy (#05081A) to a near-neutral ink, so blue becomes an accent instead of the background. The landing page's navy still sits next to it without clashing. Blue/violet goes from 4 accents to 1. Warm colors come in through paper and brass, and the semantic set the brief found missing is added.

Chakra replaces Sparkles

A 24-spoke wheel drawn in SVG strokes, so it's cheap to render. It's the loader, the daily-quota ring and the radio dial, and it's the only round decorative shape.

Paper is the second mode

Anything you'll publish or read for a while sits on paper with dark ink. At a glance, it's clear what's yours and what's the tool.

प्रकाशित DRAFT

Stamps for status

Each status has one shape: a solid stamp for published, a dashed one for draft. They work in Hindi because they don't rely on uppercase letters.

Headline on the wire
अगली ख़बर

Fewer boxes, softer edges

Cards are separated by a darker fill and a faint 1px edge rather than a hard 2px outline. Lists use thin rules instead of a box per row. Corners are 8–16px, which reads as calm rather than bubbly.

Motion: a few moments, all cheap, all optional

Story → generatorView Transitions API: the story headline slides into the generator's story panel. React Router 7 supports this through viewTransition.
The post "prints"Lines of text fade onto the paper one after another, then a stamp lands. This only animates opacity and transform, so it runs smoothly on low-end phones.
Chakra loader and quotaThe spokes light up in sequence while waiting, and one more spoke stays lit after each post.
Pressed, not litButtons darken one step and move 1px down when pressed. No glow, no scale, no hover lift.
Remove the page furnitureRemove CustomCursor, ScrollProgress and TransitionCurtain from signed-in pages, and the orbs everywhere. The splash stays on the landing page only.
Reduced motionEvery one of the above shows its final state with no animation when the user has reduced motion turned on.
03

What I saw in the running app

Too many "Soon" labels

3 of 10 sidebar items and 7 of 9 tools on the Services page say "Soon".

Dashboard leads with charts

Charts take most of the screen. Today's news, the reason people open the app, gets one card.

Orange banner everywhere

"200 posts → ₹2,000" appears as an alarm bar on top of every screen. It should be a progress card.

13-field signup

The party choice, which can never be changed, is buried between date of birth and the checkboxes.

Generator repeats itself

The profile card and "Activity score / Pro" repeat in the left column. The preview doesn't look like the platform it's for, and the news tabs get cut off on a phone.

Date locale bug

Post history shows "12 अक्टू॰ 2026" while the app is set to English.

Dead-end empty states

BhimBot opens to a blank chat. Preferences has nothing but the Reddit card.

Four shells

BhimBot, the generator and music each have their own sidebar or back button instead of the shared rail.
